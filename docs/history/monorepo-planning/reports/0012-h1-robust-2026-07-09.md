## What shipped (M16)

- **Functional-group fallback** (`functionalGroupFallback.ts`): unmatched environments get a
  rule-estimated shift by OCL-topology class instead of being dropped — acetaldehyde CHO ≈ 9.70,
  ethanol CH₂ ≈ 3.60 now appear; **nothing disappears**.
- **Per-peak provenance:** `evidence.method: "rule-estimated"` + flag; table shows "≈" + `rule-estimated`,
  figure draws a **dashed muted** stick; one `NMR_RULE_ESTIMATED` notice. A guess never reads as a match.
- **First-order split-peak rendering** in `LinkedFigureView` (recursive binomial split, J→ppm @ 400 MHz;
  resolves as you zoom, capped at 32 lines).
- **Coupling fix:** exclude labile O/N/S–H partners → ethanol CH₂ is a quartet (was a quintet).
